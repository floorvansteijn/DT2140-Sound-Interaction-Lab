    Computer test controls

    Simulation checks the mapping and sound. It does not verify phone sensors.

    How the feedback works

    Both tilt axes contribute to the angle between the phone's plane and horizontal. Enter the level state at 2° or less; leave it above 3°. This tolerance prevents rapid switching from small hand movements.

    The existing Faust brass model supplies the sound. Larger inclinations increase its blowing pressure and the repetition rate, with a separate bounded output gain.
