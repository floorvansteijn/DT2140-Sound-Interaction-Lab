    • Interaction 3: Hear if a surface is level

    Action: the phone is used to measure the angle of a surface, like a level. Sound: brass instrument.

    How to use it: tap Turn On DSP and lay the phone flat on a table, a shelf or another surface.

    What you hear: if the surface is perfectly flat, the phone stays silent. If it is tilted, you hear a brass tone. The more it tilts, the louder the tone gets. So you adjust the surface until the sound disappears, and then you know it is level.

    Why: a normal level app makes you look at the screen while you work. With sound you can keep your eyes on what you are doing, for example when hanging a shelf. It can also help people with low vision. Silence means "done", which is easy to understand without any explanation.
