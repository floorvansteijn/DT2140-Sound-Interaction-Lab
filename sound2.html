    • < Interaction 2: Point at the sky for thunder

    Action: the phone is used to point straight up. Sound: thunder.

    Pointing at the sky and hearing thunder builds on the link people already make between the sky and the weather. We imagined a weather app where you hold your phone up to the sky to check if a storm is coming. When the top of the phone points upward, thunder rumbles. It plays at most once every three seconds, so it does not repeat while you keep holding the phone up.
